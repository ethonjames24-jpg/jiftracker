JIF Budget Tracker

Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Jamaica In Focus (JIF) operates the Budget Tracker and Government Spending Explorer at tracker.infocusja.com. This policy covers the public website, tracker email subscriptions, and the Google-connected workflows used by authorized JIF operators to prepare, review, and publish updates.

The Google authorization app is managed under JIF Tracker Workflow and may appear as N8N on the Google consent screen. Visitors do not need to connect a Google account to read the tracker.

Information we handle

  • Public financial information: official government workbooks and documents, extracted figures, calculations, source links, monthly commentary, and receipts packs.
  • Subscription information: the email address you submit, consent and confirmation records, subscription status, the page and tracker month associated with a request, and submission or delivery timestamps.
  • Operator and review information: the identity of an authorized reviewer, review decisions and notes, timestamps, package versions, and records that link approval to the material published.
  • Technical information: hosting and workflow services may process IP addresses, browser or device information, request times, and error logs when providing and protecting the service.

Google account access and use

Authorized JIF operators can connect a Google account to the self-hosted n8n workflow. The permissions requested are shown on Google's consent screen. We use Google Sheets to read and update tracker, staging, review, publication, monitoring, and subscription records, and Google Drive to store and retrieve source documents, calculations, receipts PDFs, review packages, and verification records.

Google authorization tokens are handled by the server-side workflow credential system. They are not included in the public tracker frontend. The public site reads approved public financial data; it does not display private subscriber lists or private review records.

We use Google data to operate these tracker functions. We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising, data brokerage, or training generalized AI models. JIF's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Why we use information

We use information to maintain the tracker, prepare and verify monthly evidence, record human review, publish approved financial updates, process email subscription requests, send confirmation and tracker update emails, honor unsubscribe requests, and investigate operational errors or misuse.

Automated drafting may process public fiscal figures and draft commentary. Private subscriber lists are not part of the monthly drafting package. Human review remains part of the publishing process.

Service providers and public information

We use services such as Google Sheets and Drive, Hostinger for the self-hosted workflow, Cloudflare for website delivery, GitHub for the website and public receipts assets, Telegram for private operator notifications and review controls, and an email delivery provider for subscription messages. A configured AI provider may assist with drafting public financial commentary. Information is processed by these services as needed for their role; the information they receive is subject to their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

Published tracker figures, commentary, source links, and public receipts packs are available to anyone. Private operational evidence, subscriber records, and review records are maintained separately. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information where legally required or necessary to address misuse or protect the service.

Storage and retention

Records are held in the relevant Google workbooks, Drive files, and workflow or delivery systems. Public financial sources and publication evidence may be retained as an editorial archive. Personal and operational information is retained as needed to provide the service, maintain consent and unsubscribe records, investigate problems, and meet applicable obligations. Backup or service logs may remain until their normal retention cycles end.

The subscription form does not store your email address in browser local storage or session storage. This privacy page does not require JavaScript or a Google sign-in. Other pages may request public data, fonts, or assets from service providers, which can receive ordinary connection information.

Your choices

  • You can read the public tracker without subscribing.
  • You can unsubscribe using the link in a tracker update email or contact us for help.
  • You can contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with you. Some records may need to remain for consent, unsubscribe, audit, or applicable legal purposes.
  • Authorized operators can revoke the app's Google access through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future access through that authorization; it does not automatically delete records already stored. Contact us for a deletion request.

Contact and policy updates

For privacy questions or requests, email ethonjames24@gmail.com with the subject “JIF Tracker privacy”. Please provide enough information to identify the relevant record, but do not send passwords or access tokens.

We will update this page when these practices change and revise the date above.
